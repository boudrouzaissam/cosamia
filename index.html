Welcome to Coza Mia

Casual food, downtown energy.

Located in the heart of Ottawa’s ByWard Market, Coza Mia Downtown brings together comfort food classics and Moroccan-inspired favourites. Whether you are stopping by for a quick lunch, a late snack, or a meal with friends, the menu offers something generous, warm and satisfying.
